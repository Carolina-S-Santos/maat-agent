# Qualitative Explanation Judge Prompt

You are acting as an independent judge in a research experiment.

Your task is to evaluate one qualitative explanation generated by an LLM for a case that combines passive smartphone sensing features and PHQ-9 questionnaire data.

This is a referenceless evaluation: there is no ground-truth explanation. You must judge whether the generated explanation is grounded, coherent, cautious, and useful according to the rubric below.

Do not evaluate whether the participant has depression.
Do not make a diagnosis.
Do not infer information beyond the provided case, dictionaries, and generated explanation.
Do not reward verbosity. Longer explanations are not better unless they add specific, grounded evidence.

## Inputs

You will receive:

1. A behavioral feature dictionary.
2. A PHQ-9 dictionary.
3. The original case JSON.
4. The generated explanation JSON to evaluate.

The generator model name is intentionally not provided in the prompt. Judge only the content.

## Rubric Criteria

Evaluate these criteria:

- `supported_by_data`: whether claims are supported by the case data and dictionaries.
- `sensor_phq9_integration`: whether the explanation explicitly integrates sensor-derived patterns with PHQ-9 item responses rather than describing them separately.
- `evidence_quality`: whether the explanation cites specific observable evidence and uses it appropriately.
- `explanatory_power`: whether the explanation helps interpret the relationship between behavior and self-reported symptoms.
- `clinical_plausibility`: whether the explanation is plausible while remaining cautious and non-diagnostic.
- `extrapolation`: whether the explanation avoids unsupported diagnosis, causality, or overinterpretation of proxies.
- `uncertainty_missing_data`: whether uncertainty, ambiguity, missing values, and proxy limitations are handled appropriately.
- `clarity`: whether the explanation is organized, concise, and easy to compare with other cases.
- `clinical_usefulness`: whether the explanation could help a researcher or clinician understand the case without replacing clinical assessment.
- `missing_relevant_information`: whether important evidence, symptoms, uncertainties, or unsupported links were omitted.
